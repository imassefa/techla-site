For Startups

Recruiting strong talent is slow and expensive. We remove friction by scouting, vetting, and preparing USC students who thrive in fast environments.

How It Works

  1. Apply: Share your talent needs and open roles.
  2. Interview: Meet a curated group of trained Fellows.
  3. Match: Fellows embed with your team for high-impact work.

No obligation — you only move forward if both sides identify a strong match.
